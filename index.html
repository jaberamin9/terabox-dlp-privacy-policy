01Overview

TeraBox Dlp helps you fetch and save video links you already have from TeraBox. This policy explains, plainly, what the app does and doesn't collect.

There is no sign-up and no account inside the app — we don't ask for your name, email, or phone number. The only identifier the app generates is a random, anonymous ID stored on your device, described below.

In short: no accounts, no name/email/phone collection, no selling of data. The app pings an anonymous, random device ID for basic usage counts, shows ads through Google AdMob, and saves the videos you download to your device's own storage.

02Data we collect

The table below is exhaustive — this is everything the app itself sends anywhere.

WhatWhat it isTied to you?
Anonymous device ID A random ID (like a1b2c3d4-…) generated on first launch and stored only on your device. Used to count distinct active devices per day. Not identifiable
Coins-earned events A count of in-app reward coins you've earned by watching a rewarded ad, recorded against the same anonymous device ID. Not identifiable
Pasted TeraBox link Sent to our server solely to resolve a direct, playable/downloadable video URL. See Links you paste. Not identifiable
Advertising data Collected by Google AdMob to serve and measure ads. See Advertising. Google-controlled

We never collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, precise location, or the content of the files you download.

03Links you paste

When you paste or share a TeraBox link into the app, it's sent to our backend, which contacts TeraBox on your behalf to work out a direct video URL and hands that back to the app so it can play or download. The link is processed to serve that one request — we don't build a profile of what you've looked up, and requests aren't linked to your anonymous device ID.

Downloaded files are saved straight to your device's own storage (or your photo/media library, if you choose to save there) — we never keep a copy on our servers.

04Device permissions

The app requests only the permissions it needs to do the things you ask it to:

PermissionWhy we ask
Storage / media library To save the video you download to your device or photo library, and to let you share it from there.
Notifications To show you the download-in-progress notification (e.g. "Downloading… 42%") and let you know when a download finishes or fails. These notifications are generated entirely on your device — no remote push service is involved.

05Advertising

The app shows ads through Google AdMob, including optional rewarded ads you can watch to earn in-app coins. Google, as an independent controller, may collect device and advertising identifiers, approximate location, and ad interaction data to serve and measure these ads, and to personalize them where your settings allow.

You can review or limit this in your device's ad settings (Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads; iOS: Settings → Privacy → Tracking), and read Google's own practices at their Privacy Policy and Ads Settings.

06Third parties

We use a small number of infrastructure providers to run the app. None of them receive anything that identifies you personally:

  • Supabase — stores the anonymous device-ID activity ping and coin-earned counters described in Data we collect.
  • Google AdMob — serves ads, as described in Advertising.
  • TeraBox — the third-party service your pasted links point to; resolving a download means our server talks to TeraBox's servers on your behalf.

We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with anyone for their own marketing purposes.

07Retention & deletion

The anonymous device ID lives in your device's local app storage until you uninstall the app or clear its data — uninstalling removes it completely, and reinstalling generates a brand-new one. The daily-activity and coins-earned records tied to that ID are kept only in aggregate for basic usage counts, and are never combined with anything that could re-identify a person.

Pasted links are used transiently to resolve a download and are not retained afterward.

08Children's privacy

TeraBox Dlp is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us at teraboxdlp@gmail.com and we'll address it.

09Your rights & choices

Because the app doesn't collect anything that identifies you by name, there's no account data to hand over on request — but you still have control:

  • Reset your device ID at any time by uninstalling and reinstalling the app.
  • Opt out of ad personalization through your device's ad settings, as described in Advertising.
  • Ask us a question or raise a concern — including under GDPR, CCPA, or similar laws — by emailing teraboxdlp@gmail.com. If you can share your device's anonymous ID (found in the app's settings, if applicable), we can locate and delete the associated aggregate records.

10Security

We use industry-standard measures — encrypted connections (HTTPS) between the app and our backend, and access controls on our infrastructure — to protect the limited data described above. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but nothing here is designed to identify you personally in the first place.
